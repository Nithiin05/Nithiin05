## About Me

I'm a Data Analyst working on **structured data pipelines, dashboards, and predictive models**, currently interning at **Bluestock Fintech** and completing an advanced Data Science & Analytics program at **Innomatics Research Labs** (SQL, Python, Statistics, EDA, Machine Learning).

I work primarily with **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to turn raw datasets into decisions — and I'm currently deepening my skills in **Deep Learning, Computer Vision, NLP, and Generative AI**.
